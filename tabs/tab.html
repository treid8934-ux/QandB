3b · PCG 2021/4 gateways

Before the risk scoring in 3c can be used, the arrangement must pass two gateways. The arrangement is how the IPP provides services to the firm and how their share of the firm's profits is split between themselves, associates and related entities, e.g. through a family trust, bucket company, service entity or an assignment of part of a partnership interest.

Tick any statement that describes the arrangement. Leave everything unticked if none apply; an unticked gateway is passed.

Gateway 1 · Is there a genuine commercial reason for the arrangement?

Tests whether the way the IPP's profits are structured and distributed is driven by how the business genuinely operates, rather than set up mainly to reduce tax. Each statement is a sign of a lack of commercial rationale.

Gateway 2 · Does the arrangement have any high-risk features?

The ATO treats these features as high risk whatever the score. If any are present, the arrangement falls outside the scoring framework.
