Contractor Superannuation Guarantee Test (SGAA 1992 s12(3))

Because this engagement shows contractor characteristics, run this secondary ATO test to check if Superannuation Guarantee contributions are still mandatory.

1. Main Purpose is Labour

Is the contract mainly for their labour? (More than half of the dollar value of the contract is for their labour/skills, not materials or equipment).

2. Personal Labour and Skills

Paid for time/effort, not a specific result: They are paid an agreed rate for hours worked or items produced, rather than a single lump sum to achieve a one-off final project outcome.

3. Performance (No Delegation)

Must the worker perform the work personally? (The work cannot be delegated or subcontracted to someone else under the contract terms).
